# Privacy

rasterless does not collect, store, or send any data.

- Your images are processed on your own machine, or inside your AI assistant's own sandbox (Claude.ai, ChatGPT, Codex).
- The scripts make no network calls. The only downloads are the Python packages they need, fetched from PyPI on the first run, and the skill itself when you install it from GitHub.
- There are no accounts, analytics, or telemetry.

Questions: [open an issue](https://github.com/dheerajponnaganti/rasterless/issues).
